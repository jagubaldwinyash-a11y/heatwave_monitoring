System Objective

The Heat Intelligence Portal notifies citizens regarding critical heatwave levels. It aggregates meteorological insights to keep communities safe during high temperature periods.

Primary Data Feeds

System data is aggregated from IMD alerts, local weather telemetry, and satellite imagery to process actionable warnings.

Key Stakeholders

Designed for access by the general public, municipal authorities, emergency responders, and healthcare centers for early intervention.

Directive from Disaster Management Lead

Ensure continuous hydration, refrain from outdoor movement during noon hours, and regularly track regional advisories.
